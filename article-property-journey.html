REAL ESTATE · 10 NOVEMBER 2026

The people behind every property journey.

Every property decision involves people with different needs. A seeker wants a suitable place. An owner wants to reach a serious customer. An agent wants to build credibility. A developer wants to share an opportunity clearly.

A marketplace has to work for everyone.

When these needs are connected through a single platform, the property market can become more efficient and less stressful. Better discovery improves the experience for both the person searching and the person providing the property.

Truehome is being built to support these connected journeys with local relevance, accessibility and trust at its foundation.
