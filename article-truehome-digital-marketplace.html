TRUEHOME UPDATES · 10 NOVEMBER 2026

A clearer way to discover property.

Truehome is designed as one trusted space where property seekers, landlords, agents and developers can meet. It moves property discovery away from scattered, informal channels and toward a more structured digital experience.

One marketplace, many journeys.

A property seeker can search and compare. An owner can present a property to a wider audience. An agent or developer can build a professional digital presence. Each side benefits when the process is clearer and more connected.

By centring useful information and direct connections, Truehome supports better decisions for everyone involved.
