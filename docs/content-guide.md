# Content guide

For the person who keeps the website's text, people, articles and contact details up to date.
You do not need any technical knowledge. Everything is done in the admin in your web browser.

**Golden rule:** when you click Save, the change is on the public website straight away.
Read your text once before saving, then open the website to check it looks right.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to **https://trume-admin.truehome.com.ug**
2. Enter your email address and password, then click **Sign in**.
3. When finished, click **Sign out** (top right), especially on a shared computer.

- After **5 wrong passwords**, sign-in is blocked for **15 minutes** from that internet connection.
  Wait, then try again.
- Forgot your password? Ask the **owner** of the admin (the main account holder) to help.
  There is no "forgot password" email.
- To change your own name, email or password: **My account**.

## 2. What is in the admin menu

| Menu item | What it controls on the website |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Overview and the latest messages |
| **Founders** | The founder cards on the About page |
| **Insights** | The articles on the Insights page |
| **Pages** | The main heading and introduction of each of the 7 pages, and how each page appears in Google and WhatsApp/LinkedIn previews |
| **Media** | The image library (photos for founders and articles) |
| **Messages** | Enquiries sent from the Contact and Partners forms |
| **Site settings** | Email, phone numbers, address and links shown across the whole site |
| **My account** | Your own name, email and password |
| **Administrators** | Team accounts (only visible to the owner) |

The layout and design of the website (colours, sections, the service lists, etc.) are part of the
code and cannot be changed from the admin. Ask the technical person for those changes.

## 3. Formatting text

Text boxes accept a few simple marks:

| You type | The website shows |
|---|---|
| `Find your *perfect home*` | Find your ***perfect home*** — the words between asterisks appear in the lime green highlight style |
| A blank line between two paragraphs | Two separate paragraphs |
| `## Why it matters` on its own line | A subheading (in article text) |

Tips:
- Always use asterisks in pairs. One missing asterisk shows a stray `*` on the website.
- Do not paste from Word with fancy formatting; paste as plain text (Ctrl+Shift+V).

## 4. Common tasks

### Change a page's heading or introduction
1. **Pages** → click the page (e.g. *About Us*).
2. Edit the boxes under **On the page**:
   - *Small label above the main heading* — the short line in capitals at the top.
   - *Main heading* — the big title. Cannot be empty.
   - *Text under the main heading* — only on Home and Truehome.
   - *Section label / Section heading / Section text* — the introduction section below the top.
3. Under **Google and link previews**:
   - *Browser tab title* — shown on the browser tab and as the blue link in Google (keep under 60 characters).
   - *Description* — the grey text under the link in Google and in WhatsApp/LinkedIn previews (about 150 characters).
4. Click **Save and publish**, then **View page on website ↗** to check.

> If you clear a box completely, the website falls back to the original text for that box.

### Add a founder
1. **Founders** → fill in the form at the top:
   - **Name**, **Role title** (e.g. *Co-Founder & CEO*), **Focus summary** (one short line).
   - **Email** and **Phone** (optional). Several phone numbers: separate with commas.
   - **Display order** — lower numbers appear first (1, 2, 3…).
   - **Show this profile publicly** — untick to hide the card without deleting it.
   - **Photo** — choose an existing image from the list *or* upload a new one.
   - **Biography** — leave a blank line between paragraphs. The first paragraph shows on the card;
     the rest appears when a visitor clicks *View more*.
2. Click save. Use **View About page ↗** to check.

To **edit** a founder, click their name in the *All founders* table. To **hide** someone temporarily,
untick *Show this profile publicly*. **Remove** deletes them permanently.

### Publish an article (Insights)
1. **Insights** → fill in the form:
   - **Title** — `*asterisks*` work here too.
   - **Web address** — leave blank; it is created from the title (e.g. `my-article-title`).
     **Do not change it after publishing**, or links people have shared will stop working.
   - **Category** — e.g. *PROPERTY MARKET INSIGHTS* (always shown in capitals).
   - **Status** — **Draft** (hidden from visitors) or **Published** (live).
   - **Summary** — the short text on the Insights page card.
   - **Article text** — the full article. Blank lines between paragraphs; `## ` for subheadings.
   - **Image** + **Image description** — describe the photo in a few words (helps blind visitors and Google).
   - **Google description** — optional; if empty the summary is used.
2. Save. Published articles appear on the Insights page immediately, newest first.
   The newest article is shown as the large featured card.

Good practice: save as **Draft** first, check everything, then change to **Published**.
The article's date is the date it was **first published** and does not change when you edit it later.

### Change contact details (email, phone, address, links)
**Site settings** controls details that appear in several places at once:

| Setting | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Official email | Footer, Contact page — **and contact form messages are sent here** |
| Main phone number | Footer |
| Office address | Footer and Contact page. Separate parts with commas: `National ICT Innovation Hub, Nakawa, Kampala, Uganda` |
| Contact person | Contact page |
| Phone numbers on the Contact page | Contact page (separate with commas) |
| Truehome / LinkedIn / WhatsApp links | Buttons and links across the site. Must start with `https://` |

Double-check the official email: if it is wrong, form messages will not reach anyone by email
(they will still be in **Messages**).

### Upload and manage images
- Upload directly while editing a founder or article, or in advance in **Media**.
- Accepted: **JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF**, up to **8 MB**. For fast pages, keep photos under about 500 KB
  (resize to around 1200 pixels wide before uploading).
- Founder photos look best square or portrait.
- An image that is being used by a founder or article cannot be deleted — choose another image there first.

### Read and handle messages
1. **Messages** lists every enquiry, newest first. Partnership enquiries have a subject starting
   with *Partnership enquiry:*.
2. Click the email address to reply from your email program.
3. Set the status: **New** → **Read** → **Archived** to keep the list tidy.

Every message is also emailed to the official email address. If emails stop arriving, the messages
are still safe in the admin — tell the technical person.

## 5. Team accounts (owner only)

The first account (created during setup) is the **owner**. Only the owner sees **Administrators** and can:
- **Create** accounts for colleagues (name, email, a password of at least 12 characters).
  Share the password privately and ask them to change it in **My account**.
- **Disable** an account when someone leaves (they can no longer sign in), or re-enable it.

Other accounts can edit all content but cannot manage accounts.

## 6. Do's and don'ts

- ✅ Check the website after every change.
- ✅ Use strong, unique passwords and sign out on shared computers.
- ✅ Ask the technical person for a database backup before large changes (e.g. rewriting many pages).
- ❌ Don't share one account between several people — create one each.
- ❌ Don't change an article's web address after it is published.
- ❌ Don't upload very large photos straight from a phone camera without resizing.
